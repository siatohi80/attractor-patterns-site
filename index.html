SVG and DXF are in millimetres. DXF layers: BORDER, CUT, GROOVE (slot centrelines), FACETS.

Every pattern reads the same attractor field: a value from 0 to 1 per cell, strongest at the attractor and fading to zero at the influence radius. Each pattern maps that value onto its own parameter. Openings smaller than the minimum hole size are removed, and bridges are kept at least as wide as the minimum bridge where the pattern allows it. Check limits against your fabricator's specs.
