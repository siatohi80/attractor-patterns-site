How the field works

Each opening reads a value from 0 to 1: strongest at an attractor, fading to zero at the influence radius. The falloff sets the shape of that fade, and each pattern maps the value onto its own property (step 3). Openings below the minimum hole size are removed, and bridges never go below the minimum bridge. Check limits against your fabricator's specs.

Sizes are in millimetres. The sheet is 640 × 360 mm; exports keep that scale.
